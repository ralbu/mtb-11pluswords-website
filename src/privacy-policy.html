Privacy Policy

11+ Words app are provided by Mind that Bit Ltd. This policy explains how we handle information.

Our Approach

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no login required to use the app.

11+ Words is built to be simple and safe to use, especially given its audience of children preparing for the 11+ exam. Anything the app remembers - which words have been practised, scores, and settings - is stored locally on the device. If you uninstall the app, that information is deleted with it.

Children's Privacy

We take children's privacy seriously. The app:

  • Does not require any personal information to use
  • Has no chat, messaging, or social features
  • Does not collect names, ages, locations, or any identifying information
  • Is safe for children to use without adult supervision from a privacy standpoint

Because we do not collect personal information in the app, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 - or from anyone else. If you believe we have somehow received personal information about a child, please contact us and we will delete it.

Advertising

The app is free and contains no third-party advertising. We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not sell, rent, or share data with ad networks or data brokers.

Google Play

The app is distributed via the Google Play Store. Downloading and installing the app is subject to Google's own privacy policy, which governs your relationship with Google Play. We have no access to any data Google may collect as part of that process.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights over personal data held about you, including the right to access it, correct it, or have it deleted. In practice we hold no personal data about you, so there is generally nothing to access or delete. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, uninstall it or clear its data in your device's app settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example if we introduce new features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at google_play@mindthatbit.com.
